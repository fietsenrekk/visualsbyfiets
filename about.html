Visuals by Fiets is a one-person VFX & motion studio built on a single obsession: making people stop scrolling.

In a feed where everything looks the same, I cut edits for artists, creators and brands who want a real visual signature: beat-synced, typography-heavy, engineered frame by frame for TikTok, Reels and Shorts.

The punch-in lands exactly when the 808 does. That is not a style. That is the standard.

Next to editing I model and animate 3D logos with cinematic materials (chrome, gold, glass) and I sell the actual project files, because the fastest way to level up is to open a finished edit and see how it ticks.

No agencies. No handoffs. You brief me, I cut it, we refine it together.
